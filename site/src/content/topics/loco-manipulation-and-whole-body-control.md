## Definition and scope

Loco-manipulation and whole-body control (WBC) covers commanding every actuated joint of a legged humanoid so that locomotion, balance and object interaction are solved as one coupled problem rather than as separate subsystems. A controller here must maintain contact feasibility at the feet, regulate centre-of-mass and angular momentum, and simultaneously realise a task objective such as carrying, pushing, opening or bracing against contact.

The category is distinguished from Locomotion (where the objective is a base velocity or terrain traverse and the arms are largely free) and from Manipulation (where the base is fixed or treated as a decoupled mobile platform). Papers land here when the arms disturb balance, when the legs extend reach or apply force, or when one controller exposes a unified command interface across both. The list holds 179 entries, from 2022 to mid-2026, dominated by learning-based methods on commercial humanoid platforms.

## Why this problem matters

A humanoid that only walks, or only manipulates from a stationary stance, recovers little of the morphology's advantage. Lifting a box off the floor, pulling a cart, opening a spring-loaded door or reaching a high shelf all require the legs and torso to supply reach, stability margin and reaction force the arms cannot. The difficulty is that the underactuated floating-base dynamics that make balance hard also make manipulation forces destabilising: a pull at the hand is a torque about the support polygon.

## Historical development

The classical formulation assumed an accurate rigid-body model and treated WBC as constrained optimization. Pattern generators reduced the humanoid to a linear inverted pendulum tracking a zero-moment-point reference ([Kajita et al., ICRA 2003](https://mzucker.github.io/swarthmore/e91_s2013/readings/kajita2003preview.pdf), historical reference, not in the list), while hierarchical task frameworks composed prioritised objectives — balance above posture above reaching — into one instantaneous solve ([Sentis and Khatib, IJHR 2005](https://www.worldscientific.com/doi/abs/10.1142/S0219843605000594), historical reference, not in the list). Hierarchical quadratic programming made those stacks fast enough to close the loop ([Escande, Mansard and Wieber, IJRR 2014](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0278364914521306)), and the DARPA Robotics Challenge exposed their brittleness under model error and perception noise ([Johnson et al., JFR 2017](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/rob.21674)) — both historical references, not in the list. The assumptions were explicit: known kinematics and inertias, few pre-planned contacts, largely quasi-static motion, and evaluation by demonstration on one machine.

That tradition did not disappear; it became cheaper and less model-exact. Sampling-based predictive control removed the need for analytic derivatives and ran on a general simulator ([Predictive Sampling](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/predictive-sampling-real-time-behaviour-synthesis-with-mujoco), [Whole-Body MPC with MuJoCo](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/whole-body-model-predictive-control-of-legged-robots-with-mujoco)), and contact-mode search plus whole-body MPC handled doors and drawers on a legged platform ([Sleiman et al., Science Robotics 2023](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/scirobotics.adg5014), historical reference, not in the list). Within this list, optimization mostly survives as a reference generator: [Opt2Skill](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/opt2skill-imitating-dynamically-feasible-whole-body-trajectories-for-versatile-h) uses differential dynamic programming for dynamically feasible, contact-consistent trajectories and trains RL to track them, reporting that torque-carrying references improve contact-force tracking over inverse-kinematics references.

The decisive change was massively parallel GPU simulation and sim-to-real RL, which traded model accuracy for domain randomisation and volume ([Rudin et al., CoRL 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.11978), historical reference, not in the list). Two lineages converged. From graphics came motion-imitation RL, where a tracking reward on mocap references yields natural motion without hand-designed gait objectives ([DeepMimic, SIGGRAPH 2018](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.02717); [Perpetual Humanoid Control, ICCV 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.06456)), enabled by large mocap corpora ([AMASS, ICCV 2019](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.03278)); from robotics came unified legged manipulation policies ([Fu et al., CoRL 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.10044)) — all historical references, not in the list. In this list the transition appears in [Sim-to-Real Learning for Humanoid Box Loco-Manipulation](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/sim-to-real-learning-for-humanoid-box-loco-manipulation) and then in [Expressive Whole-Body Control](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/expressive-whole-body-control-for-humanoid-robots), which lets the upper body imitate a retargeted human reference while the legs track velocity — an asymmetric split adopted because mocap references are not dynamically feasible for the robot's legs. State representation changed accordingly, from centroidal quantities and ZMP to proprioception plus a reference-motion embedding, with privileged information reserved for a simulation teacher. [WoCoCo](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/wococo-learning-whole-body-humanoid-control-with-sequential-contacts) pushed the other way, decomposing tasks into contact stages so that task-agnostic rewards suffice with no motion prior.

A second shift concerned where data comes from. Because human motion was the supervision signal, teleoperation became a data engine rather than a demo. [H2O](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/learning-human-to-humanoid-real-time-whole-body-teleoperation) built a retargeted dataset by filtering motions through a privileged imitator, then trained a real-time tracker deployable from an RGB camera; [OmniH2O](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/omnih2o-universal-and-dexterous-human-to-humanoid-whole-body-teleoperation-and-l) and [HumanPlus](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/humanplus-humanoid-shadowing-and-imitation-from-humans) closed the loop, using shadowing to collect on-robot demonstrations for behaviour cloning from egocentric vision. Instrumentation then diversified to cut operator cost: an isomorphic exoskeleton cockpit in [HOMIE](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/homie-humanoid-loco-manipulation-with-isomorphic-exoskeleton-cockpit), mocap-free consumer VR in [TWIST2](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/twist2-scalable-portable-and-holistic-humanoid-data-collection-system), a wearable exoskeleton in [HumanoidExo](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/humanoidexo-scalable-whole-body-humanoid-manipulation-via-wearable-exoskeleton), and by 2026 robot-free capture in [Humanoid Manipulation Interface](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/humanoid-manipulation-interface-humanoid-whole-body-manipulation-from-robot-free) and [EgoHumanoid](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/egohumanoid-unlocking-in-the-wild-loco-manipulation-with-robot-free-egocentric-d). A plausible reading is a sustained attempt to remove the robot from the data-collection loop.

Architecture then consolidated. Rather than one policy per task, [HOVER](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/hover-versatile-neural-whole-body-controller-for-humanoid-robots) argued that full-body kinematic imitation is a common abstraction underlying navigation, loco-manipulation and tabletop control, and distilled several command modes into one policy; [HugWBC](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/hugwbc-a-unified-and-general-humanoid-whole-body-controller) added gait parameters and an upper-body intervention channel to the command space. Scaling the tracking objective followed: [GMT](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/gmt-general-motion-tracking-for-humanoid-whole-body-control) used adaptive sampling and a motion mixture-of-experts to cover a diverse motion manifold with one policy, [UniTracker](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/unitracker-learning-universal-whole-body-motion-tracker-for-humanoid-robots) and [From Experts to a Generalist](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/from-experts-to-a-generalist-toward-general-whole-body-control-for-humanoid-robo) consolidated by distillation, and [SkillBlender](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/skillblender-towards-versatile-humanoid-whole-body-loco-manipulation-via-skill-b) blended primitive skills hierarchically to reduce per-task reward engineering. [SONIC](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/sonic-supersizing-motion-tracking-for-natural-humanoid-whole-body-control) states the scaling hypothesis explicitly: networks from 1.2M to 42M parameters, over 100M motion frames, roughly 21k GPU hours, with performance improving as compute and data diversity grow.

Once trackers became strong, the binding constraint moved to the references. [ASAP](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/asap-aligning-simulation-and-real-world-physics-for-learning-agile-humanoid-whol) attacked residual dynamics mismatch between simulator and hardware; [OmniRetarget](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/omniretarget-interaction-preserving-data-generation-for-humanoid-whole-body-loco) argued that conventional retargeting produces foot-skating and penetration and discards human-object and human-environment contact, using an interaction mesh to preserve it and reporting long-horizon parkour and loco-manipulation on a Unitree G1 from policies with five reward terms and shared domain randomisation. [Retargeting Matters](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/retargeting-matters-general-motion-retargeting-for-humanoid-motion-tracking), [SPIDER](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/spider-scalable-physics-informed-dexterous-retargeting) and [DynaRetarget](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/dynaretarget-dynamically-feasible-retargeting-using-sampling-based-trajectory-op) form a 2025-2026 sub-literature whose premise is that data quality, not policy capacity, is the bottleneck.

The manipulation half of the term arrived late and mainly through force. Kinematic tracking says nothing about interaction wrenches, so [FALCON](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/falcon-learning-force-adaptive-humanoid-loco-manipulation) introduced a dual-agent scheme whose force curriculum escalates end-effector force within torque limits, validated on payload transport, cart-pulling and door-opening. Related lines add tactile sensing ([TACT](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/tact-humanoid-whole-body-contact-manipulation-through-deep-imitation-learning-wi), [distributed tactile multi-contact control](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/whole-body-multi-contact-motion-control-for-humanoid-robots-based-on-distributed)), bulky-object embracing ([Embracing Bulky Objects](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/embracing-bulky-objects-with-humanoid-robots-whole-body-manipulation-with-reinfo)), explicit compliance ([SoftMimic](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/softmimic-learning-compliant-whole-body-control-from-examples), [GentleHumanoid](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/gentlehumanoid-learning-upper-body-compliance-for-contact-rich-human-and-object-)), and residual correction over a general tracker ([ResMimic](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/resmimic-from-general-motion-tracking-to-humanoid-whole-body-loco-manipulation-v), [SEEC](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/seec-stable-end-effector-control-with-model-enhanced-residual-learning-for-human)).

In parallel the loop closed around perception and language. Blind proprioceptive policies gave way to vision-conditioned ones — [VisualMimic](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/visualmimic-visual-humanoid-loco-manipulation-via-motion-tracking-and-generation), [PhysHSI](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/physhsi-towards-a-real-world-generalizable-and-natural-humanoid-scene-interactio) and [VIRAL](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/viral-visual-sim-to-real-at-scale-for-humanoid-loco-manipulation), the last reporting that scaling simulation to tens of GPUs with heavy visual randomisation was necessary for reliable RGB transfer. Above them, vision-language interfaces appeared: [Harmon](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/harmon-whole-body-motion-generation-of-humanoid-robots-from-language-description) and [LangWBC](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/langwbc-language-directed-humanoid-whole-body-control-via-end-to-end-learning) for language-conditioned motion, and [LeVERB](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/leverb-humanoid-whole-body-control-with-latent-vision-language-instruction), which learns a latent action vocabulary from rendered kinematic demonstrations to drive an RL whole-body policy, reporting 58.5% overall success on its own 150-task benchmark. Generative and behavior-foundation formulations followed: guided diffusion over a tracking latent in [BeyondMimic](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/beyondmimic-from-motion-tracking-to-versatile-humanoid-control-via-guided-diffus), unsupervised forward-backward representations in [BFM-Zero](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/bfm-zero-a-promptable-behavioral-foundation-model-for-humanoid-control-using-uns), and a [survey](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/a-survey-of-behavior-foundation-model-next-generation-whole-body-control-system-) framing the direction.

Evaluation practice changed alongside. Early papers reported qualitative demonstrations; 2025-2026 entries increasingly use standardised stress tests — table tennis ([HITTER](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/hitter-a-humanoid-table-tennis-robot-via-hierarchical-planning-and-learning)), soccer, badminton, extreme balance ([HuB](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/hub-learning-extreme-humanoid-balance)) — and treat failure as a first-class object of study, through getting-up policies ([Learning Getting-Up Policies](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/learning-getting-up-policies-for-real-world-humanoid-robots), [HiFAR](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/hifar-multi-stage-curriculum-learning-for-high-dynamics-humanoid-fall-recovery)) and protective falling ([SafeFall](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/safefall-learning-protective-control-for-humanoid-robots), [Robot Crash Course](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/robot-crash-course-learning-soft-and-stylized-falling)). Household-scale benchmarking appears in [BEHAVIOR Robot Suite](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/behavior-robot-suite-streamlining-real-world-whole-body-manipulation-for-everyda).

## Major technical transitions

- Model-based optimization gives way to sim-to-real RL; model accuracy is traded for simulated volume.
- Trajectory optimization survives as a reference generator, not a runtime controller ([Opt2Skill](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/opt2skill-imitating-dynamically-feasible-whole-body-trajectories-for-versatile-h), [AMO](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/amo-adaptive-motion-optimization-for-hyper-dexterous-humanoid-whole-body-control)).
- Data moves from hand-designed gaits to human mocap, then teleoperated on-robot demos, then robot-free egocentric video.
- Command interface moves from per-task policies to multi-mode controllers to promptable latent spaces.
- Objective moves from kinematic tracking to force- and compliance-aware interaction.
- Observation moves from proprioception to depth, RGB and scene geometry ([MeshMimic](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/meshmimic-geometry-aware-humanoid-motion-learning-through-3d-scene-reconstructio), [LessMimic](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/lessmimic-long-horizon-humanoid-interaction-with-unified-distance-field-represen)).
- The perceived bottleneck shifts from policy architecture to retargeting and data quality.
- Evaluation shifts from single demonstrations to task suites, sports and failure-mode studies.

## Comparison of dominant approaches

| Approach family | Data requirement | Generality | Robustness | Sim-to-real cost | Typical failure mode |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Model-based WBC / whole-body MPC | None; needs an accurate model and contact schedule | Retargetable by re-specifying costs; new contact modes need planning | Good near the modelled regime | Low, no simulator gap to cross | Model mismatch, unmodelled compliance, contact-schedule brittleness |
| Motion-tracking RL from human mocap | Large retargeted mocap corpora; quality dominates quantity | Broad over motion style, weak over object interaction | Strong to perturbation after randomisation | High: parallel GPU sim plus randomisation and residual alignment | Infeasible references; long-horizon drift; contact ignored |
| Decoupled or multi-agent RL with force curricula | Command and force schedules rather than demonstrations | Good for payload and reaction-force tasks, narrower in style | Explicitly trained against interaction wrenches | Moderate; curriculum design cost | Coordination loss between halves; torque saturation |
| Hierarchical latent / behavior foundation models | Tracking-scale data plus task or reward prompts | Highest nominal generality; one policy, many prompts | Inherited from the low-level tracker | High; an extra stage above a costly controller | Latent commands unreachable for the low-level policy |
| End-to-end visuomotor / VLA from teleoperation | On-robot demos, or human video plus robot post-training | Strong semantic generality, sensitive to scene shift | Depends on visual randomisation and demo coverage | Highest in sim; otherwise high human data cost | Visual distribution shift; compounding long-horizon error |

## Current research frontier

Recent entries cluster around four threads. First, foundation-model formulations of control: promptable latent policies ([BFM-Zero](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/bfm-zero-a-promptable-behavioral-foundation-model-for-humanoid-control-using-uns)), end-to-end language-action models ([SENTINEL](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/sentinel-a-fully-end-to-end-language-action-model-for-humanoid-whole-body-contro), [FRoM-W1](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/from-w1-towards-general-humanoid-whole-body-control-with-language-instructions)), latent VLA ([WholeBodyVLA](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/wholebodyvla-towards-unified-latent-vla-for-whole-body-loco-manipulation-control)) and open loco-manipulation foundation models ([Ψ₀](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/0-an-open-foundation-model-towards-universal-humanoid-loco-manipulation)). Second, scaling and data recipes ([data and model scaling](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/unveiling-the-impact-of-data-and-model-scaling-on-high-level-control-for-humanoi), [SUGAR](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/sugar-a-scalable-human-video-driven-generalizable-humanoid-loco-manipulation-lea), [ZeroWBC](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/zerowbc-learning-natural-visuomotor-humanoid-control-directly-from-human-egocent)). ## Open problems and unresolved limitations

Force and contact remain under-specified relative to motion. Most tracking objectives are kinematic, and the papers that add force ([FALCON](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/falcon-learning-force-adaptive-humanoid-loco-manipulation), [HAFO](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/hafo-a-force-adaptive-control-framework-for-humanoid-robots-in-intense-interacti), [FAME](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/fame-force-adaptive-rl-for-expanding-the-manipulation-envelope-of-a-full-scale-h)) mostly do so through curricula rather than measured contact-force feedback; whole-body tactile sensing is still rare.

Long-horizon composition is unresolved. Hierarchical systems ([Being-0](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/being-0-a-humanoid-robotic-agent-with-vision-language-models-and-modular-skills), [Task and Motion Planning for Humanoid Loco-manipulation](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/task-and-motion-planning-for-humanoid-loco-manipulation), [HiWET](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/hiwet-hierarchical-world-frame-end-effector-tracking-for-long-horizon-humanoid-l)) split the problem, but the interface between semantic planner and dynamics-level policy remains a source of infeasible commands, as the [LeVERB](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/leverb-humanoid-whole-body-control-with-latent-vision-language-instruction) benchmark numbers suggest.

Evaluation is not yet comparable across papers: differing robots, retargeting pipelines and task definitions mean reported success rates rarely support direct comparison, and shared benchmarks remain recent and unevenly adopted.

Finally, reliance on human motion data carries an embodiment assumption that [Ψ₀](/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning/papers/0-an-open-foundation-model-towards-universal-humanoid-loco-manipulation) challenges directly, arguing that naive co-training on human and humanoid data is suboptimal given kinematic disparities. Whether human video is best used as pretraining signal, as reference motion, or as neither is a question the 2026 entries answer differently.
